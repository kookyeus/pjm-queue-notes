A project filing in 2003 was operating in eighteen months. One filing in 2017

took five years.

The part that is easy to misread

The 2019 and 2020 cohorts show shorter medians — 4.4 and 3.2 years. That is not

an improvement. It is survivorship. Only the fastest projects from those

cohorts have completed at all; the slow ones are still in the queue or have

withdrawn, and they cannot appear in a median of completions.

Read those two rows as a floor, not a trend.

What it means together with Note 002

A 2017 filing faced roughly 85% odds of never being built, and five years to

find out. A 2003 filing faced 64% odds and eighteen months.

The cost of a queue position is not the interconnection fee. It is the option

value of five years of capital and site control, spent on a coin that now lands

badly five times in six.
